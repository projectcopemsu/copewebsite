# COPE Photo Guide

Name each photo **exactly** as shown, then upload it to `assets/img/` in GitHub. It appears on the site automatically, no code changes.

- Lowercase, dashes, no spaces. `.jpg` for photos, `.png` for logos.
- Bigger is fine. The site crops to fit, so keep the main subject in the center.
- Square slots (grants, events) look like tall boxes on computers and wide boxes on phones, so a square photo crops best.
- Under 500 KB per photo keeps the site fast. Compress at squoosh.app or tinypng.com.
- Missing a photo? The striped placeholder stays until you add it.
- Newsletter covers and Local Legends honoree photos are added later in `data.js`.
