Our mission

Empowering the community to build a safe, healthy, and drug-free Paterson.

Individuals

Culturally tailored substance use, HIV/AIDS and viral hepatitis health promotion for underserved populations.

Community

Dismantle the social structures that promote underage alcohol and drug use, and reduce youth access to alcohol and tobacco.

Policy

Research and youth-led advocacy that changes local policy, like Paterson’s Tobacco 21 ordinance.
